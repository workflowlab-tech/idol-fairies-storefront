## What happens after I place an order?

After an order is successfully placed, it proceeds through the
applicable order processing and fulfillment stages.

## Can I change an order after placing it?

Changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has been placed. Contact the
store as soon as possible if a change is needed.

## Can product availability change during ordering?

Yes. Availability can change, particularly for limited or high-demand
merchandise.

## What happens if an ordered product cannot be fulfilled?

If a fulfillment problem affects an order, the customer should be
contacted or provided with the applicable resolution based on the
circumstances.
