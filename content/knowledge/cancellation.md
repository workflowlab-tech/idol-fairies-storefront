## Cancelling an in-stock order

An in-stock order can be cancelled any time before it's marked as packed or
dispatched. Once it has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled — at that
point you would need to go through the Returns & Refunds process instead
once you receive it.

## Cancelling a preorder

Preorders follow a different, shorter window — see the Preorder Policy for
details (cancellable within 24 hours of ordering, locked in after that).

## How to request a cancellation

Contact us with your order details as soon as possible after ordering. The
sooner you reach out, the more likely your order is still within the
cancellable window.
