## Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?

Yes. Idol Fairies offers reseller and bulk pricing for shops, fan groups,
and events. Submit the Wholesale & Bulk Orders inquiry form on the
homepage with the products and estimated quantity you're interested in,
and the store will follow up directly.

## Is there a minimum order size for wholesale?

Yes. Wholesale and bulk pricing applies starting at a 20-piece minimum
per order.

## How do wholesale inquiries work?

Wholesale orders are handled by inquiry, not through the regular
checkout. Submitting the Wholesale & Bulk Orders form does not place an
order or create a charge — it sends a request for the store to review
and follow up on with pricing, availability, and next steps.

## Can Idol AI give me a wholesale price or place a wholesale order?

No. Idol AI can explain that wholesale and bulk pricing exists, the
20-piece minimum, and direct you to the Wholesale & Bulk Orders inquiry
form. It cannot negotiate or quote wholesale pricing, and it cannot
create a wholesale order — a team member handles that after reviewing
your inquiry.
