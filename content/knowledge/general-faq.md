## What is Idol Fairies?

Idol Fairies is a K-pop merchandise store offering albums, light sticks,
photobooks, magazines, collectibles, and other official merchandise.

## Are the products official?

Products listed as official merchandise are sourced from established
K-pop merchandise suppliers and retailers. Check the individual product
page for product-specific information.

## Do you have a physical store?

Idol Fairies operates as an online store.

## How can I find a specific product?

Use the website search and category pages, or ask Idol AI. You can
provide an artist, group, product name, album, merchandise type, or
other details.

## Can Idol AI answer in different languages?

Yes. Idol AI can assist customers in multiple languages. Product names
and certain official terms may remain in their original form.
