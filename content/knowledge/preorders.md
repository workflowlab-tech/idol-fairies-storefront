## What is a preorder?

A preorder allows customers to order eligible merchandise before its
regular availability or release.

## When will my preorder ship?

Timing depends on the product's release, supplier availability, arrival,
and order processing. Check the individual product information for
relevant release or preorder details.

## Are preorder dates guaranteed?

No. Release dates and supplier schedules can change.

## Can preorder availability end?

Yes. Preorders may close or sell out depending on supplier availability
and allocation.

## Can I cancel a preorder?

Cancellation eligibility depends on the order status and applicable
store policy. Contact the store before assuming that a preorder can be
cancelled.

## Can I combine preorder and in-stock products?

Availability and fulfillment may differ when an order contains products
with different release or availability schedules. Check the applicable
order information before purchase.
