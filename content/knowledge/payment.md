## Current payment status

This storefront is a front-end portfolio demo, so no live payment gateway
is connected yet — the cart is for browsing and demonstration purposes
only, and no real charges are made.

## Planned payment methods

A production version of Idol Fairies PH would support common Philippine
payment methods: GCash, Maya, major credit/debit cards, and bank transfer,
all charged in Philippine Pesos (PHP).

## Currency

All prices on the site are listed in PHP (₱). We don't currently support
other currencies at checkout.
