## Are these official/authentic items?

Yes — every item we sell is guaranteed authentic and sourced directly from
Korea. We source from established K-pop retail suppliers and list catalog
details (artist, version, price, availability) as provided by that source.
If a listing's exact inclusions matter to you before ordering, check the
product page's dispatch note or ask Idol AI.

## Will a sold-out item restock?

It depends on the item and its source supplier. Some sold-out versions,
especially limited or early-bird editions, never restock. We don't guess a
restock date we haven't actually been given — ask Idol AI, and it will tell
you honestly if that information isn't available yet.

## How do I contact support?

Use the "Ask Idol AI" chat button anywhere on the site for quick answers on
products, shipping, preorders, or policy. For anything Idol AI can't
resolve, it will let you know that a human follow-up is needed — human
escalation and order lookup are planned for a future phase of this project.

## Do you have a physical store?

No — Idol Fairies PH currently operates online only, with orders shipped
via courier nationwide within the Philippines.
