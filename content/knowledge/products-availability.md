## What does In Stock mean?

In Stock means the product is currently listed as available for
purchase.

## What does Preorder mean?

Preorder means the product is being offered before its regular
availability or release according to the product listing.

## What does Sold Out mean?

Sold Out means the product is currently unavailable for purchase.

## Can a sold-out item become available again?

A sold-out product may become available again if additional stock is
obtained, but restocking is not guaranteed.

## How do I check availability?

Check the current status displayed on the product page. Idol AI may also
help locate products and explain the status shown in the catalog.

## Can availability change?

Yes. Product availability may change as inventory and supplier
availability change.
