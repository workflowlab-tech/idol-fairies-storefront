## What can Idol AI help with?

Idol AI can help customers find products and answer questions about
product availability, wholesale/bulk orders, shipping, orders, returns,
and other documented store policies.

## Can Idol AI find products?

Yes. Customers can ask about artists, groups, albums, light sticks,
photobooks, magazines, collectibles, and other products available in the
catalog.

## Does Idol AI know current product availability?

Idol AI should rely on the current product catalog when answering
product-specific availability questions.

## Can Idol AI make up unavailable information?

No. If reliable information is unavailable, Idol AI should say that the
information cannot be confirmed rather than inventing an answer.

## Are Idol AI answers final for order-specific problems?

No. Issues involving a specific payment, order, refund, damaged
shipment, cancellation, or other account-specific matter may require
store review.
