## What "Preorder" means

A product marked Preorder has not yet arrived in our inventory — it is
reserved against an upcoming official release or an expected restock from
our supplier. Placing a preorder guarantees you a unit once stock arrives;
it does not mean the item ships immediately after you order it.

## When preorders ship

Preorder items ship at least 20 days after the item's official release date
in Korea — this is the minimum time our supplier needs to fulfill stock to
us after release, so treat 20 days as the earliest possible dispatch, not a
guarantee. Some items take longer depending on supplier availability. Check
the product page's release date and dispatch note for the most current
information we have on file, or ask Idol AI.

## Cancelling a preorder

Preorders can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of placing the
order. After that window, the preorder is considered locked in on our end,
because we have already committed to purchasing that unit from our supplier
on your behalf, and it can no longer be cancelled.

## Preorder payment

Preorders are paid in full at the time of ordering, the same as an in-stock
purchase — we do not currently offer a deposit-only preorder option.

## Mixed orders with a preorder item

If your order combines an in-stock item with a preorder item, we hold the
entire order until the preorder item is ready, so everything ships together
in one parcel. You can ask us to split the shipment instead, though this may
add a separate shipping fee for the second parcel.
