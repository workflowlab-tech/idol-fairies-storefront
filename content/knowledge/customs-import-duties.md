## No customs on domestic delivery

Idol Fairies PH ships from within the Philippines to Philippine addresses
only. Because delivery is entirely domestic, customers never pay customs or
import duties on their order — those only apply to cross-border shipments,
which we don't do.

## Where our stock comes from

Some of the merchandise we sell is originally imported from Korea by our
own suppliers before it reaches our inventory. Any import cost on that side
is already factored into the price you see listed — there is no additional
fee charged to you at delivery.
