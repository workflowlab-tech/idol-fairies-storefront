title: Payment
---

## What currency are prices displayed in?

Use the currency and price currently displayed by the storefront or
checkout for the applicable purchase.

## What payment methods are accepted?

Customers should use the payment methods currently presented during
checkout. Idol AI should not promise a payment method that is not shown
by the checkout system.

## When is an order confirmed?

An order should only be considered successfully placed after the store's
checkout or order system confirms it.

## What if my payment fails?

If payment is unsuccessful, check the payment information and available
payment options before trying again. Do not submit repeated payments if
it is unclear whether the original transaction succeeded.
