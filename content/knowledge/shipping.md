## Where does Idol Fairies ship?

Available shipping destinations are determined by the shipping options
presented during checkout or by the store's current shipping
arrangements.

## How much is shipping?

Shipping cost depends on the order and available delivery method. The
applicable shipping charge should be confirmed during checkout.

## When will my order ship?

Processing and dispatch depend on product availability and order status.
Preorder products may require additional time because they depend on
release and supplier schedules.

## How do I track my order?

When tracking information is available for an order, customers should
use the tracking information provided for that shipment.

## Can shipping be delayed?

Yes. Carrier, supplier, customs, weather, peak-period, and other
logistical delays can affect delivery.

## Does Idol Fairies guarantee a delivery date?

No. Estimated delivery information should not be treated as a guaranteed
arrival date.
