## Where we ship

Idol Fairies PH ships from the Philippines and delivers nationwide within
the Philippines only, via local courier partners (J&T Express, LBC, or
Ninja Van, chosen based on your delivery area and parcel size).

## No overseas shipping

We do not offer international or overseas shipping of any kind. Every order
must use a Philippine delivery address — we cannot ship to addresses outside
the Philippines.

## Delivery timelines

Once an order is dispatched, Metro Manila addresses typically receive their
parcel within 2–4 business days. Addresses outside Metro Manila (provincial,
island, or remote areas) typically take 4–8 business days, depending on the
courier's coverage in that area. These are courier transit estimates, not
guarantees — weather, holidays, and courier volume can extend them.

## Dispatch timing for in-stock items

In-stock items are packed and handed to the courier within 1–3 business days
of payment confirmation. The delivery timeline above starts counting from
dispatch, not from the order date.

## Shipping cost

Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and
the total weight/size of your order. We don't publish one flat shipping
rate because a single album costs far less to ship than a bundle of light
sticks or multiple photobooks.

## Order tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive the courier's tracking number
so you can follow the parcel on the courier's own tracking page.
